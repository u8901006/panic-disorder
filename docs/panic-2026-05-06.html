Behaviour Research and Therapy · Instructed increase in fear activation during exposure exercises does not enhance treatment effects of cognitive behavioral therapy for panic disorder and agoraphobia.

這項124人的RCT直接挑戰「暴露時應最大化患者恐懼」的常見臨床建議，發現額外加入內感受性挑戰以增加恐懼激活，不僅沒有提升療效，在治療結束時甚至表現較差，但兩組在四個月追蹤時效果相當。

O治療後與四個月追蹤之主要結果指標（恐懼症狀、逃避行為等）

Psychological Medicine · Exposure-based cognitive behavioral therapy delivered by assertive community treatment teams for severe mental illness with symptoms of anxiety: a cluster randomized controlled trial

這項15個ACT團隊的群集RCT證實，將結構化暴露治療整合到ACT服務中，可顯著降低嚴重精神疾病患者的特質焦慮（d=-0.60至-0.64），並改善功能與生活品質，且具成本效益。

Journal of Global Health · Innovative virtual reality exposure therapy for anxiety and posttraumatic stress disorder: a meta-analysis of randomised controlled trials
