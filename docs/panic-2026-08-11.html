The international journal of neuropsychopharmacology · Ketamine effects on EEG and their links to therapy differ across treatment-resistant major depression, post-traumatic stress disorder, and obsessive-compulsive disorder.

酮胺於治療抵抗性創傷後應激症患者誘導特定腦電波（特別是alpha頻帶）變化，而於重度抑鬱及強迫症則無此效應，提示其治療機制具疾病特異性。
