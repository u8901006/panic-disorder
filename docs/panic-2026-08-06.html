SSM - Mental Health · Feasibility and acceptability of the co-adapted Early Adolescent Skills for Emotions (EASE) intervention for South African adolescents with symptoms of depression or anxiety, and their caregivers

該研究驗證了一項經文化適應的、由照護者參與的青少年情緒技能訓練方案（EASE）在南非資源有限環境中的可行性與高接受度，顯示其作為低成本、可擴展之早期焦慮預防介入的潛力。
