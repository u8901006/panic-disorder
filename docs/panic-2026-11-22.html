Defensive reactivity to interoceptive perturbation in panic disorder: Depressive symptom burden and panic severity dimensions.

Translational psychiatry · Defensive reactivity to interoceptive perturbation in panic disorder: Depressive symptom burden and panic severity dimensions.

恐慌症患者於高氧負擔任務後，防禦反應持續增強；其中抑鬱症嚴重程度是預測防禦反應最強的因子，優於恐慌症嚴重程度，揭示了抑鬱症負擔在恐慌症防禦機制中關鍵作用，提供了恐慌症與抑鬱症共病的生物學機制見地。
